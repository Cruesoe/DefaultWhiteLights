[h1]Default White Lights[/h1]
Vanilla electric lights default to orange. This mod makes them white, and lets you choose a different default per room type.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*] Standing lamps, wall lamps, flood lights, and other colourable electric lights default to [b]white[/b] instead of orange.
[*] Optionally assign a colour to each room type. A light built in that room uses that colour.
[*] Unticked rooms use the global default.
[*] The in-game colour picker and Ideology darklight toggle still work.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[b]Options → Mod options → Default White Lights[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]All lights[/b] — global default colour (white unless you change it). Click the colour box for the vanilla colour wheel.
[*] [b]Room colours[/b] — tick a room and click its colour box to override the default for lights built there.
[*] [b]Apply to all existing lights[/b] — recolours colourable electric lights already on the map to match the current settings, using the room they are in now. Load a colony first.
[/list]
New lights take the colour when they finish building. Lights you have already recoloured by hand keep that colour until you hit Apply.

[h2]What it does not change[/h2]
Sun lamps, torches, campfires, and other glowers that cannot be recoloured in vanilla are left alone.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] RimWorld 1.6
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]
[/list]
Safe to add to an existing save.

[i]Load after Harmony.[/i]
